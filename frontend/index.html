How it works

                    
From photo to medicine list

                

            


            

                

                    01
                    
Upload or drop

                    
Take a photo with your phone or drag in a scan. JPG, PNG, WEBP, BMP and TIFF are supported.

                

                

                    02
                    
OCR reads the page

                    
The image is straightened, cleaned and upscaled, then Tesseract extracts every line of text.

                

                

                    03
                    
Details are organized

                    
Medicine names, strengths, doses, frequency and course length are pulled into a structured summary.
